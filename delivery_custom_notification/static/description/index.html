Delivery Custom Notification

Odoo 14.0 Inventory Delivery Email Automation

Overview

Send custom delivery notification emails to designated contacts when deliveries are validated. This module provides carrier-level control over notification emails, with support for custom templates and alternative email servers.

Key Features

  • Delivery Contact per Partner: Configure a specific contact who will receive all delivery notifications
  • Carrier-Specific Templates: Each delivery method can have its own notification template
  • Intelligent Fallback: Automatically sends to delivery contact if set, otherwise to main partner
  • Alternative Mail Servers: Templates can be configured to use services like Mailgun
  • Tracking URL Support: Include dynamic tracking links in notifications (compatible with 2Ship and others)
  • Selective Application: Only affects configured carriers, preserving standard behavior for others

Use Cases

  • Send delivery notifications to warehouse contacts instead of accounting contacts
  • Use different notification templates for different carriers (standard, express, freight)
  • Integrate with third-party tracking systems via custom template URLs
  • Route delivery emails through dedicated transactional email services
  • Provide customers with branded tracking experiences

Configuration

  1. Partner Setup: Set the Delivery Contact in the Sales & Purchase tab
  2. Carrier Setup: Enable notifications and select template in the Notifications tab
  3. Template Creation: Create custom email templates with your branding and tracking URLs
